# About UltraRide

## Find your line.

Ultra-distance cycling is booming. Every year new events appear: a gravel crossing of the Alps, a self-supported tour of Brittany, a loop through Morocco's Atlas mountains. But finding them means digging through dozens of organiser websites, Facebook groups and forums.

UltraRide brings everything together in one place: a free, clear and independent directory of ultra-distance cycling events in Europe and North Africa. Road, gravel or MTB, fully self-supported or assisted, over one night or three weeks.

## Our principles

- **Free, for everyone.** Browsing, searching and listing an event costs nothing, for participants or organisers.
- **Independent.** No event pays to be ranked higher. The display order depends on your filters, not on an advertising budget.
- **At the service of organisers.** Every listing links to the event's official website, and registration always happens with the organiser. An organiser can take over their listing at any time.
- **Respectful of your data.** No ads, no trackers, data hosted in France.
