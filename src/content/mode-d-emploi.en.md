# How it works

UltraRide is free to browse, no account needed. A free account unlocks favourites, reviews and your race record; an organiser account lets you manage your own events.

## Looking for your next ultra

**Search.** Type an event name, a country, a region or an organiser in the search bar. Accents and capital letters don't matter.

**Filter.** Narrow down by discipline (road, gravel, MTB), format (race, adventure, endurance), country, support, route type, month and open registrations. Filters combine; "Reset" clears them all.

**Near me.** Turn on location to see starts within 400 km. Your position stays on your device: it is neither sent nor stored.

**The map.** Each dot is a start, coloured by discipline. Zoom in to split grouped events, tap a dot to open its listing.

**An ultra's listing.** Distance, elevation gain, dates, start and finish locations, track and profile when available, description, participant reviews and organiser contacts. The "Register" button takes you straight to the organiser.

> The information is indicative: always check the dates, rules and conditions on the official website before signing up.

## You ride: your participant account

**Create your account.** Tap "Create an account", choose the Participant profile and confirm your address with the email you receive. Your password must be at least 12 characters long, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a digit.

**Your favourites.** Tap the star on a listing to keep it handy. You'll find all your favourites in your space.

**Your reviews.** Have you ridden an event? Leave a rating and a comment on its listing. Every review is checked before it is published, and if you edit a published review it goes back for checking. Reviews must describe a real experience.

**Your race record and Ultra Passport.** Record the ultras you've finished: date, distance, elevation gain, time and track. Your record grows event after event, and you can export it as a PDF Ultra Passport.

**Your data.** You can edit or delete your account at any time from your space. Newsletters are only sent to you if you have accepted them.

## You organise: your organiser account

**Create your account.** Choose the Organiser profile when signing up. Then complete your organiser profile: organisation name, website, contact email, social networks, logo and description. These details appear automatically on your events' listings.

**Is your event already on UltraRide?** Probably: we list events based on public information. Write to [hello@ultraride.eu](mailto:hello@ultraride.eu) from your organisation's address and we'll link your listings to your account.

**Add an ultra.** From your space, fill in the listing: name, discipline, format, support, route type, dates, distance, elevation gain, start and finish locations, description, image and registration link.

**Validation.** Every new listing, and every change to an existing one, waits for validation before it is published. We check that the information is consistent and publish the listing within 48 hours at most.

**Images.** Accepted formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, 5 MB maximum. Only use visuals you own the rights to.

**Remove an event.** You can ask for a listing to be deleted from your space, giving the reason. It is handled quickly.

> Keep your listings up to date: dates, prices and registration openings are what participants look at most.

## Glossary

| Criterion | Value | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discipline | Road | Mostly tarmac, road bike |
| Discipline | Gravel | A mix of roads and rideable tracks |
| Discipline | MTB | Trails and technical terrain |
| Format | 🏁 Race | Timed, with a ranking |
| Format | 🧭 Adventure | No race, no ranking: a route to enjoy at your own pace, with or without a provided track |
| Format | ⏰ Endurance | A repeated loop, unlimited laps or over a set time |
| Support | 🎒 Self-supported | No outside help: you handle food, sleep and mechanics on your own |
| Support | 🧳 Semi-supported | A few support points provided (food stops, drop bags, base camps) |
| Support | 🚚 Supported | Organised support or a follow vehicle allowed |
| Route | 🔁 Loop | Start and finish in the same place |
| Route | 📌 Point to point | Finish in a different town from the start |
| Route | 🔄 Out and back | Ride to a turnaround point, then back to the start |

The exact rules vary from one event to another: the organiser's rules always prevail.
